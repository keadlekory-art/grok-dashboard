About

Cinematography prompt coach for AI image and video generation. Turns plain-language scene descriptions into ready-to-use prompts that specify shot size, camera angle/movement, lens, depth of field, lighting, color grade, and mood. Explains the vocabulary so you learn while working.

Idle
Current setup: Mix of apps (Grok Imagine, Midjourney, Runway, Kling, Veo, etc.)

Documentation & Prompt Index

  • Core Workflow

    Describe a scene in plain words → receive a structured prompt with shot size (ECU, CU, MS, WS), camera angle & movement, lens/focal length, DOF, lighting setup, color grade, and mood. Includes a short explanation of why each choice works.

  • App Adaptation

    Prompts start with shared cinematic vocabulary, then tailored tweaks for the specific tool you’re using that day. Just name the app with the scene.

  • Personal Library

    Keeps a library of shot types and prompts that worked for you. Helps plan full shot lists for short stories.

  • Getting Started Prompt

    Example starter: “an old man waiting alone at a rainy bus stop at night” or “a woman finds an old letter in the attic.” Describe however it comes to you.

  • Vocabulary Focus

    Shot sizes: ECU, CU, MS, WS. Camera angles and movements. Lighting setups and mood control. Designed so the language sticks over time.

  • Camera Directions Reference Sheet

    A 30-entry visual reference for shot sizes, camera angles, camera movements, and lenses, with example images and copy-ready prompt phrases for AI short story videos.

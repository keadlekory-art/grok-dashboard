    Three separate reference sheets for Bob, made on November 21, 2026 after a first combined sheet put every turnaround view and pose facing front. Bob is bald with a short white beard, no glasses (removed from the source photo without changing facial structure), an off-white tunic with dark patterned trim, a charcoal-gray patterned himation, and brown leather sandals. The turnaround has labeled front, 3/4, side, and back views. The pose sheet shows four distinct poses: seated on a stool with a goblet, standing and holding grapes, leaning forward at a wooden table, and a raised-goblet toast. The face sheet shows front, profile, and 3/4 views plus six expressions in costume (Neutral, Amused Chuckle, Deadpan, Wry Smile, Big Laugh, Thoughtful). One noted miss: Deadpan does not show a raised eyebrow clearly. For video reference, use the face sheet for close-ups and the turnaround for wide shots.

  • AI Video Limitations & Workarounds

    Full 360° orbits cause robotic reverse playback. Recommended alternatives: 90° arcs, lateral tracking shots, or cutting multiple arcs in edit. Face and costume consistency improves with shorter, one-direction moves.

  • Character Preservation Notes

    Bob, laurel-wreathed man, and salt-and-pepper man held faces and costumes better on the 45–60° arc than full orbit. Wreath stayed intact. Clapping action remains stiff — suggestion to switch to swaying/raising cups.

  • Current Recommendation

    Prefer the 90° arc take. Offer to re-render with looser dancer actions or save to Greek-Storyboard folder.
